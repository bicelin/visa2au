## If something goes wrong

Our internal complaint process is set out in clause 14.1 of the Agreement: we acknowledge a complaint within **2 business days** and give a substantive written response within **14 business days**.

You may also raise a concern directly with an external statutory body at any time:

| Body | Handles | Contact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Office of the Migration Agents Registration Authority (OMARA) | Conduct of registered migration agents | [mara.gov.au/complain](https://www.mara.gov.au/complain/make-a-complaint) · 1300 881 995 |
| Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC) | Consumer guarantees and fair trading | [accc.gov.au](https://www.accc.gov.au) · 1300 302 502 |
| Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) | Privacy and personal information | [oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au) · 1300 363 992 |

Decisions on a visa application are made by the Department of Home Affairs, not by us. If a visa is refused, any review of that decision is a separate engagement — merits review before the **Administrative Review Tribunal** and judicial review are expressly outside the scope of the Service Agreement (clause 2.2).
