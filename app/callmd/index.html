Call.md — Meeting Copilot Pilot

A review of video-db/call.md — an open-source (MIT) desktop meeting assistant by VideoDB (the AI division of Daily.co) — and what it means for Visa2AU consultations.

What Call.md actually is

Call.md is a local-first Electron desktop app — a copilot that sits beside any meeting you take on your Mac (Zoom, Google Meet, MS Teams, a Daily.co consultation) and: records screen + microphone + system audio, transcribes both sides in real time (you vs. them), shows live AI coaching and research results during the call, and produces a summary with action items when it ends. It is not a video platform and not a web service: there is no room, no WebRTC, nothing to host on a server. Meetings still happen on Daily.co; Call.md watches and assists.

Capabilities

During the call

Dual-channel live transcription (language selectable), live-assist “say this / ask this”, talk-ratio & WPM metrics, monologue detection, coaching nudges in an always-on-top widget.

MCP auto-triggering

Detects information needs in conversation and calls your configured MCP tools mid-call (results panel inline) — e.g. look up a visa subclass or client record.

After the call

Three-part AI summary (narrative, attributed key points, action items), Markdown export with transcript + metrics, webhook push to n8n / Zapier / CRM at meeting end.

Before the call

Google Calendar sync, meeting-setup wizard (AI discussion checklist + probing questions from the meeting description).

Call.md vs. our Daily.co pipeline

Daily.co and Call.md are complements, not substitutes — both from the same company family (Daily → video-db/VideoDB).

Privacy & compliance notes

  • Transcription audio flows to VideoDB’s cloud (US infra) — client consent wording for consultations should cover it, same as Daily recording.
  • Local-first storage: history/transcripts live in a local SQLite DB, keys in the OS secure store; 2-hour recording cap; SSRF-guarded webhooks; clean logout.
  • MARN duty of care: any live-assist suggestion is aid, not advice — agent remains responsible for content given to clients.

Getting started on an agent Mac

  1. Install: curl -fsSL https://artifacts.videodb.io/call.md/install | bash
  2. Get a free VideoDB API key at console.videodb.io and register it in the app.
  3. Grant Microphone + Screen Recording permissions when prompted.
  4. Start “New Meeting” before joining a Daily consultation; review the post-call export before any client-facing use.
