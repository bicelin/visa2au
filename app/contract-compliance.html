Contract Compliance

Every clause of the Visa2AU Service Agreement, mapped to the Australian legislation that governs it.

English · Русский · Français · English is the authoritative version.

About this page

This page is the clause-to-legislation cross-reference referred to in clause 1.6 of the Visa2AU Pty Ltd Service Agreement for Immigration Assistance. It covers the December 2026 edition of the Agreement, which is the edition we currently issue, and it was last reviewed on 23 November 2026.

It records, clause by clause, the Commonwealth instruments that govern each part of the Agreement, so that a client can see why each term exists and which regulator stands behind it.

Where anything on this page differs from the Agreement you have signed, the signed Agreement prevails. This page is a summary of legal instruments only: it is not legal advice, it does not vary your Agreement, and it does not exclude or limit any consumer guarantee under the Australian Consumer Law.

How to read this table

  • Clause — the numbered clause of the Service Agreement (December 2026 edition).
  • Subject — what that clause actually does in the engagement.
  • Governing legislative provision — the Commonwealth Act, regulation or Code provision the clause implements.

Instruments this Agreement works to

  • Migration Act 1958 (Cth)
  • Migration Regulations 1994 (Cth)
  • Migration Agents Regulations 2026 (Cth)
  • Migration (Migration Agents Code of Conduct) Regulations 2026 (Cth) — “the Code”
  • Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth), including the Australian Consumer Law (Schedule 2)
  • Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), including the Australian Privacy Principles (Schedule 1)
  • Electronic Transactions Act 1999 (Cth) and Copyright Act 1968 (Cth)
