Every clause of the Visa2AU Service Agreement, mapped to the Australian legislation that governs it.

About this page

This is the clause-to-legislation cross-reference referred to in clause 1.6 of the Visa2AU Pty Ltd Service Agreement for Immigration Assistance. It records, clause by clause, the Australian legislative provisions that govern each part of that Agreement.

It is maintained as a convenience for clients and for the transparency of our engagement terms. Where anything on this page differs from the Agreement you have signed, the signed Agreement prevails. This page is a summary of legal instruments only and is not legal advice.

Our regulatory framework

All immigration assistance provided by Visa2AU Pty Ltd is given by, or under the direct supervision of, Registered Migration Agents (RMAs) registered with the Office of the Migration Agents Registration Authority (OMARA) and bound by the Migration (Migration Agents Code of Conduct) Regulations 2021.

If something goes wrong

Our internal complaint process is set out in clause 14.1 of the Agreement: we acknowledge a complaint within 2 business days and give a substantive written response within 14 business days.

You may also raise a concern directly with an external statutory body at any time:

BodyHandlesContact
Office of the Migration Agents Registration Authority (OMARA)Conduct of registered migration agentsmara.gov.au/complain · 1300 881 995
Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC)Consumer guarantees and fair tradingaccc.gov.au · 1300 302 502
Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)Privacy and personal informationoaic.gov.au · 1300 363 992

Decisions on a visa application are made by the Department of Home Affairs, not by us. If a visa is refused, any review of that decision is a separate engagement — merits review before the Administrative Review Tribunal and judicial review are expressly outside the scope of the Service Agreement (clause 2.2).
